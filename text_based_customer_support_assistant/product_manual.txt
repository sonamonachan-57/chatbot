SMART HOME WIFI ROUTER – PRODUCT MANUAL

PRODUCT NAME:
Smart Home WiFi Router

PRODUCT OVERVIEW:
Smart Internet WiFi works by continuously monitoring your network and making real-time adjustments to optimize performance. By collecting data on your usage habits, the system predicts when and where you need the strongest signal, ensuring a fast and reliable connection at all times.

PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS:
Wi-Fi router specifications define network performance with key metrics including:

* Wi-Fi 6 / Wi-Fi 7 standards (802.11ax / 802.11be)
* Dual-band / Tri-band frequencies (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz / 6 GHz)
* Gigabit WAN and LAN ports

TROUBLESHOOTING:

Router Unable to Access the Internet:
If the router cannot access the Internet:

1. Verify that the Internet connection is working
2. Check whether the wireless router can obtain an Internet IP address

Internet Connection Check:

* Ensure your cable or ADSL modem has a successful connection
* Check modem status lights for connection confirmation
* Refer to modem documentation or contact your Internet service provider if needed

Wireless Router Internet Light Status:
Green or Blinking Green:
Indicates a stable Internet connection and correct wiring

Internet Light Off:

* Ensure the Ethernet cable is securely connected between modem and router
* Confirm both modem and router are powered on

SAFETY INFORMATION:

Radiation Level:
Wi-Fi routers emit low-level non-ionizing RF electromagnetic fields (EMF), far below international exposure limits.

Signal Weakness:
Signal strength decreases rapidly with distance, meaning RF exposure drops significantly beyond three feet.

Children:
There is no established evidence that low-level RF from Wi-Fi affects children; however, placing routers away from sleeping areas is recommended.

Recommended Distance:
For added safety and comfort, keep the router 6–10 feet (1–2 meters) away from areas where people spend long periods.
